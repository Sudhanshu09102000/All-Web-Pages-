Common questions

How do I check my typing speed?

Open Speed test, choose 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes and type the words on the screen. When time runs out you get your WPM, KDPH, accuracy and your most-missed keys.

How is WPM calculated?

Every 5 correctly typed characters (spaces included) count as one word. WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. KDPH is correct characters per hour, so 35 WPM equals 10,500 KDPH.

What is a good typing speed?

Under 25 WPM is beginner, 30–40 is average, 45–60 is good and anything above 60 is fast. In job and exam tests accuracy counts as much as speed, so aim for 95% or higher.

How do I get faster as a beginner?

Learn touch typing. Rest your fingers on the home row (A S D F and J K L ;), let each finger press only its own keys, and keep your eyes on the screen. Practise 30 minutes twice a day and use Weak keys to fix your slip-ups.
