PixelForge · Complete GuideWhat it does, what it doesn’t, and how it protects your files

✨ What is PixelForge?

PixelForge is an all-in-one toolkit that converts, compresses, resizes, scans and reads text from your images, PDFs and documents. Everything runs inside your own browser, so your files are never uploaded to any server.

🔒 Privacy & security

  • Files are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Device Rooms send files directly between your devices, encrypted with a room password.
  • No account, no sign-up, no tracking of your files.
  • OCR language data and a few libraries are downloaded once from public CDNs, so an internet connection is needed the first time.

🛠 What you can do

  • Compress to an exact size: set a target in KB or MB; optionally pad up to the limit.
  • Resize: scale %, exact width × height, or longest side; set DPI.
  • Adjust: rotate, mirror, black & white, brightness, contrast, background colour.
  • Scan & Crop: auto-detects the page edges, straightens perspective and offers 12 scan modes. PDFs are edited page by page.
  • Extract text (OCR): read text from photos and scans, or the digital text inside PDFs. Export as PDF, TXT, Word or JSON. Many languages supported.
  • Select pages: pick a range like 1-5, 8 and make a new PDF, with an optional size limit.
  • Merge & split: combine files into one PDF or PPTX, or split a PDF into one card per page.
  • Organise: drag to reorder, reverse order, rename, per-file Undo / Redo, copy settings to many files.
  • Save & share: download one file, Save all, or one ZIP (with an optional size limit).
  • Device Rooms: send files between phone and computer with a code and password. No cable, no app.
  • Look & feel: light, dark or auto theme, accent colours, adjustable card size.

⚠️ Good to know (limits)

  • Text conversions (PDF to DOCX, ODT, EPUB…) keep the text, not the original layout.
  • PPTX files are redrawn in the browser, so complex designs may look simplified.
  • TIFF and GIF output, and PDF/A, are not available (they need a server engine).
  • OCR accuracy depends on image quality. Clear, well-lit, straight photos work best.
  • Non-Latin text in generated PDFs may need an internet connection for fonts.
  • Password-protected PDFs and very large files may not open, depending on your device’s memory.
  • Device Rooms need both devices online; some networks block direct links (a relay can be added).

💡 Quick tips

  • Tick the round checkbox on cards to apply a button or setting to only those files.
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Ctrl+A selects all cards.
  • Drag the corner grip on a card, or use the Card size slider, to resize previews.
  • Photographing a document? Use Scan with camera for the best result.
