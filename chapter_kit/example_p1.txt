N trap: Percentages of <i>different bases</i> can never be added directly.
M: a + b + ab⁄100 — “<b>A</b>dd <b>B</b>oth, then <b>A</b>dd <b>B</b>y hundred”
@@Q A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net change?
1) 6% increase  2) 8% increase  3) 10% increase  4) 12% increase
ANS: 2
EXP: ✅ 8%: 20 − 10 + (20 × −10)/100. 📌 Check: 100 → 120 → 108. ❌ 10% forgets the ab/100 term.
@@Q Sugar price rises by 25%. By what percent must consumption fall to keep expenditure the same?
1) 20%  2) 25%  3) 15%  4) 30%
ANS: 1
EXP: ✅ 20%: 25/125 × 100. 📌 Price ↑ 1/4 → use ↓ 1/5. ❌ 25% ignores the new base.
