THE WORD PAGE (what build.py makes from your lines)
Part-of-speech pill, meaning card, Hindi meaning card, synonyms card, antonyms card, 3 usage sentences (the word in bold), one picture with a card "Sentence shown in the picture" (the chosen usage sentence with the word highlighted), and the optional memory hook. The page is responsive and every size follows the portal's font-size control. You only write the content lines above.

CONTENT RULES
- Meaning: the real, current dictionary meaning in the sense the exam uses most, in plain words a beginner understands (max 12 words). If my list gives a meaning that is wrong or unclear, silently write the correct one and note it in "changelog".
- Part of speech: NOUN, VERB, ADJECTIVE, ADVERB, PHRASE, IDIOM or ONE WORD (for a one-word substitution). Spell it in full, never "n." or "adj.".
- Hindi meaning: natural, correct Hindi in Devanagari, the way a teacher would say it; 1-3 short terms.
- Synonyms and antonyms: real dictionary synonyms and antonyms of the SAME sense and part of speech; include a few easy words and the exam-favourite words. Never put the word itself, a different sense or a mere related word in them.
- Usage sentences: exactly 3, natural, 8-16 words, three different situations, each makes the meaning clear from context (a learner should be able to guess the meaning). Mark the word (or its changed form, for example [[garrulously]]) with [[ ]] in all three.
- Picture (PIC): choose the sentence that is easiest to draw (usually 1). Write ONE concrete scene in 25-45 words: who is there, where, what they are doing, their expressions, the key objects, and any short text that must be visible (spelled exactly). The scene must show the MEANING at a glance. No abbreviations. Never ask for text overlay, captions or the word itself on the picture. Never draw the picture yourself (no SVG, no ASCII art). For people and faces use ordinary invented characters; no real or famous persons, no brands.
- Memory hook: only for words with a clear root, prefix, suffix or a true sound-link. Never invent a false etymology.

LANGUAGE (very important)
Never mention the source anywhere (pages, questions, options, explanations, overview, YouTube text, picture briefs). Banned in any form: "according to the notes", "as given in the list", "in the word list", "in the book", "the text says", "as sir said", "as discussed in class", "from the PDF" and any similar phrase that points to a list, book, notes, PDF, lecture or teacher. State every fact directly. build.py and validate.js reject these phrases and show the line.
Write for a complete beginner: simple words, short full sentences. No abbreviations in sentences: write "for example" not "e.g.", "that is" not "i.e.", "and so on" not "etc.", "percent" not "%". Exam tags such as SSC CGL may stay.

QUESTIONS (SSC CGL Tier 1 and Tier 2 level)
Exactly 2 questions per word, right after its word page. Moderate difficulty with believable options, in the wording style of the real paper. Do not copy a past question word for word.
- Question 1: "Select the most appropriate synonym of the given word." or "Select the most appropriate antonym of the given word." Put the word, written with a capital first letter, on the W: line. Use a synonym question for odd-numbered words and an antonym question for even-numbered words; if the word has no antonym, ask the synonym question.
- Question 2 (a different type from question 1; pick the one that suits the word best):
  a) Fill in the blank (default): head "Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank." and the NEW sentence with one blank "______" on the W: line. The sentence must be different from the three usage sentences and must contain a clue, so exactly one option fits.
  b) One-word substitution: head "Select the most appropriate one-word substitute for the given group of words." and the group of words on the W: line (for words that are one-word substitutes).
  c) Idiom or phrase meaning: head "Select the option that best expresses the meaning of the given idiom/phrase." and the idiom on the W: line (for IDIOM or PHRASE entries).
  d) Spelling: "Select the correctly spelt word." (only for words that are commonly misspelt).
- Options: exactly 4 different, believable options of the same part of speech and a similar length. In synonym questions include one antonym and one look-alike or sound-alike word as traps. In antonym questions include one synonym as a trap. In fill-in-the-blank questions every wrong option must be a real word that makes the sentence wrong in meaning, not in grammar. No "all/none of the above". Write ANS with the true correct option; build.py reshuffles the options so the correct answers are spread evenly over 1-4, so never mention option numbers or letters in the explanation.
- Explanation: plain text, 2-3 short full sentences, max 45 words: why the right option is correct (you may add the Hindi meaning in brackets), one easy way to remember it, and why the tempting wrong option fails. No HTML, no abbreviations.
